Sources

Where the items in 22 issues come from: 393 citations across 83 sites.

This is a weekly curated digest for pastors and elders. Each issue draws on the last seven to ten days, and every item links to the report it rests on. We prefer a news report over an organization’s own page, and the tone is reported with discernment, not appetite.

A few notes on method. WORLD is gated, so we write only from what is visible without a subscription. Baptist Press items are often read through the Missouri Pathway republication but cited as Baptist Press. Founders and Grimké items are framed as announcements, not news.
